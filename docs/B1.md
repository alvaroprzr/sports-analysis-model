# B1: gradient boosting on player-ball physics with a three-state chain decoder

B1 is the reserve submission: a complete, reproducible pipeline trained and tuned on external
data. BAR-ATH is only predicted: nothing is trained or tuned on it
(configuration configs/b1_ext.json).

## Labelled data used

| source | what | how much | labels by |
|---|---|---|---|
| IDSSE / Sportec open DFL data (Bassek et al. 2025, *Sci. Data* 12:195, CC-BY 4.0) | ball status and owning team per frame | 5 development matches for training and selection; 2 held out for one final check | the data provider (DFL) |
| SkillCorner open data (github.com/SkillCorner/opendata @ 4340d27, MIT) | team in possession from SkillCorner's phases of play | 17 development matches; 3 held out | SkillCorner's own models (no published accuracy): used with half the weight and only in live play |

No label of the provided match is used, for training or for validation.

## Pipeline (scripts/make_submission.py)

1. **Simulator calibration** (src/poss/simulate.py and src/poss/sim_external.py). The calibration
   code is applied to three SkillCorner development matches (upsampled to 25 fps, undetected
   players missing): ball and player smoothing, track cuts, position snaps, ball gaps with hazard
   models. Each training copy draws its own intensity (domain randomisation: visible ball
   U(20 %, 100 %) through a table measured on clean DFL, track-cut hazard x U(0, 3)). Choice of
   this arm: experiments/sim_ext_select2.md.
2. **Training rows** (src/poss/dataset.py). DFL development matches, two simulated degradations each
   (each at its own intensity, as above), plus team-swapped copies; SkillCorner development
   matches as recorded, plus team swap. Player-ball physics (src/poss/physics.py) is computed at
   the native rate and aggregated to 10 Hz; temporal context is added as means over centred,
   past and future windows.
3. **Two classifiers** (LightGBM, deterministic, fixed seed and threads): P(alive) on DFL only
   (SkillCorner's dead ball is approximate) and P(home | alive) on DFL + SkillCorner live frames.
4. **Decoding** (src/poss/decode.py): Viterbi over H / A / D at 10 Hz with three costs, then each
   decision is held over the nearest 25 fps frames; frames outside play are X.

## Choices and where they come from

| choice | value | evidence |
|---|---|---|
| simulator arm | domain randomisation | best mean over SkillCorner at three ball-visibility levels, 14 matches not used to calibrate (experiments/sim_ext_select2.md) |
| team-switch cost | 3 | zone score over simulated DFL (both readings of the labels) and SkillCorner live (experiments/b1_ext_dr_costs.md) |
| stoppage costs | 6, 6 | no effect between 2 and 12 |
| SkillCorner weight | 0.5 of DFL in the team model and in the selection score | agreed in advance; scores always reported per source |
| working rate | 10 Hz | labels resampled to 10 Hz keep spellF1 0.995 (experiments/dataset_audit.md) |

Development scores (5-fold CV, cost chosen excluding the evaluated fold): DFL simulated 0.480
(second reading of the labels 0.630), SkillCorner live 0.789. The decoder settings and the physics constants are documented in the module docstrings.

## Reproduction

    python scripts/fetch_dfl.py
    git clone https://github.com/SkillCorner/opendata.git data/external/skillcorner_raw/repo
    git -C data/external/skillcorner_raw/repo checkout 4340d274572876239c154c90bc507a9b3250a656
    python scripts/fetch_skillcorner.py --lfs && python scripts/fetch_skillcorner.py
    python scripts/make_submission.py --team <team> --config configs/b1_ext.json

CPU only; about an hour on 6 cores. The script prints the SHA-256 of the file; three runs from
empty caches give the same file.
